# Candidate feature ledger

All six main acts, water, six difficulty ranks and continuing 30-performance shows are playable. Water includes source dolphin integration and schedules; the candidate also adds finish celebrations, conditional lion rewards, the money-hoop ladder and consistent green shading.

[ARCADE-FIDELITY.md](ARCADE-FIDELITY.md) records exact coverage and known differences, including unimplemented special hidden rewards. [validation.md](validation.md) records what was tested. Historical C64 reports do not authorize differences from the arcade target.

The optional six-panel arcade chooser allows five performances of each act before marking it NO MORE. Completing all six five times opens a harder show. Building with `--chooser automatic` removes selection entirely; no non-arcade chooser remains. See [chooser.md](chooser.md).
