# Upright victory animation validation

The new authored Charlie poses face the audience, keep the head, torso and
feet fixed, and wave both raised arms. The lion remains planted on the platform.

`release.json` identifies both complete cartridge builds. `reproducible.json`
checks regeneration of prepared assets and byte-identical cartridge rebuilds.
The acceptance reports cover all 34 CPU/pixel suites for each profile.

The natural-win celebration reports measure the actual rendered animation
and recorded audio on NTSC and PAL. The accompanying PNGs are emulator output.
The trampoline reports cover three roof-hit losses, both retries and continue
expiry on each standard. Whole-game reports cover every demonstration scene;
the hardest NTSC trapeze route separately checks the tight rendering deadline.

See `docs/validation.md` for test scope and reproduction commands.
